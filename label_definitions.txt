informed: makes a claim and backs it with something a reader could check, such as a number, a version, a named source or link that supports the point, or a concrete account of something the poster did or saw
hot_take: states an opinion, verdict, prediction or general assertion about the topic without offering anything a reader could check
chatter: makes no claim about the topic, such as a genuine question, a joke, thanks or agreement, a bare link, or a remark about the conversation itself
