informed: makes a claim and backs it with something a reader could check, such as a number, a version, a named source or link that supports the point, or a concrete account of something the poster did or saw, or whose main point is itself a specific checkable fact
hot_take: states an opinion, verdict, prediction, recommendation or general assertion about the topic without offering anything a reader could check
chatter: makes no assertion about the topic, such as a genuine question, a joke, thanks, a personal feeling or status update, a bare link, or a remark about the conversation itself
